for CatQuest Extended voice packs. It does not redistribute the full audio packs.

CatQuest Extended is an independent community continuation of
[CatQuest by Cathey](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/catquest).

The published Russian voice pack is attributed and licensed separately according
to its pack metadata. See the corresponding release attribution before reusing or
redistributing audio.
